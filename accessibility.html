Accessibility Statement

Last updated: March 2026

Our Commitment

Gummy Clouds Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We believe that every family — regardless of disability — should be able to access our dental care tools, educational resources, and services. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide array of disabilities. Conforming to these guidelines helps us ensure that our website and application are accessible to the broadest possible audience.

While we strive to adhere to the accepted guidelines and standards for accessibility, it is not always possible to do so in all areas of the website. We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our digital properties.

Measures Taken

Gummy Clouds takes the following measures to ensure accessibility:

  • Include accessibility as part of our design and development process from the earliest stages.
  • Provide text alternatives for all non-text content, including images, icons, and illustrations.
  • Ensure all interactive elements are keyboard accessible and provide visible focus indicators.
  • Maintain sufficient color contrast ratios across all themes (light, dark, brand, and clouds modes).
  • Use semantic HTML and ARIA landmarks to support screen reader navigation.
  • Provide captions and transcripts for video and audio content in our educational resources.
  • Conduct regular accessibility audits using both automated tools and manual testing with assistive technologies.
  • Train our team on accessibility best practices and inclusive design principles.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of Gummy Clouds. If you encounter accessibility barriers on any part of our website or application, please let us know:

  • Email: accessibility@gummyclouds.com
  • Phone: +1 (800) 555-GUMMY
  • Contact form: gummyclouds.com/contact

We try to respond to accessibility feedback within 3 business days and to propose a solution within 10 business days.

Assessment Approach

Gummy Clouds assesses the accessibility of our digital properties through the following methods:

  • Self-evaluation using WAVE, axe DevTools, and Lighthouse accessibility audits.
  • Testing with assistive technologies including VoiceOver (macOS/iOS), NVDA (Windows), and TalkBack (Android).
  • Annual external accessibility audits conducted by third-party experts.
  • Usability testing with participants who have diverse abilities and use various assistive technologies.

5 Ways to Make Brushing Fun for Toddlers

Dr. Sofia Martinez

/

Mar 25, 2026

/

6 min read

Getting toddlers excited about brushing their teeth can feel like an impossible task. Between the squirming, the protests, and the occasional toothpaste-flavored meltdown, many parents dread the twice-daily routine. But it does not have to be this way.

At Gummy Clouds, we believe that dental care should be a joyful part of every child's day. After working with hundreds of families and consulting with pediatric dentists, we have distilled the most effective strategies into five simple approaches that truly work.

1. Turn It Into a Story

Children are natural storytellers. Instead of saying "brush your teeth," try framing it as a mission: "The sugar bugs are hiding in your mouth, and only your magic toothbrush can chase them away!" This narrative approach taps into your child's imagination and gives them a reason to participate actively.

Our cloud buddies in the Gummy Clouds app do exactly this. Each brushing session becomes a new chapter in an ongoing adventure, with characters that grow and evolve based on your child's consistency.

2. Let Them Choose Their Tools

Autonomy is a powerful motivator, even for two-year-olds. Let your child pick their own toothbrush color, choose between flavors of toothpaste, or decide which song plays during brushing time. These small choices give them ownership over the experience.

3. Make It Musical

Music transforms everything. A two-minute brushing song gives kids a clear sense of how long to brush while making the experience feel like a dance party rather than a chore. You can use popular kids' songs or even make up your own brushing anthem.

"Children who associate brushing with positive experiences are 3x more likely to maintain good dental habits into their teenage years."

-- Dr. Emily Chen, Pediatric Dentistry Journal

4. Brush Together as a Family

Kids learn by imitation. When they see Mom and Dad brushing with enthusiasm, they want to join in. Make brushing a family activity, standing side by side at the bathroom mirror. You can even have friendly competitions about who makes the most bubbles.

5. Celebrate Small Wins

Positive reinforcement goes a long way. Whether it is a sticker chart on the bathroom wall, a special bedtime story after brushing, or Gummy Stars in the app, recognizing your child's effort builds lasting motivation.

Here are some quick-win reward ideas that families love:

  • Create a weekly sticker chart with a small reward at the end
  • Let them pick a bedtime story after a great brushing session
  • Use the Gummy Stars system to unlock fun digital badges
  • Have a special "brushing champion" dance after each session
  • Take a monthly photo to show their "sparkling smile" progress

The Bottom Line

Making brushing fun is not about tricks or bribes. It is about creating a positive association that lasts a lifetime. When children see dental care as an enjoyable part of their day, they carry that attitude with them as they grow.

Try one or two of these strategies this week, and watch how your child's attitude toward brushing starts to shift. And if you want a little extra help, our cloud buddies are always ready to make the journey even more magical.

Dr. Sofia Martinez

Pediatric dentist and family health advocate with 12 years of experience. Dr. Martinez is passionate about making dental care accessible and enjoyable for children of all ages. She contributes regularly to the Gummy Clouds blog.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent support matters, our response time is usually under 4 hours.

Can I schedule a demo for my dental practice?

Absolutely! Select "Dental professional inquiry" in the contact form above and we'll set up a personalized demo for your practice. We offer both virtual and in-person walkthroughs.

Do you offer partnership opportunities?

Yes! We partner with pediatric dental clinics, schools, healthcare organizations, and children's brands. Reach out through the form with "Partnerships" selected and we'll share our partnership information.

Is my data safe when I submit this form?

Your privacy is important to us. All form submissions are encrypted and handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We never share your information with third parties without your consent.

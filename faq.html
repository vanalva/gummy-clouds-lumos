Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Gummy Clouds. Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out to our team.

General

What is Gummy Clouds?

Gummy Clouds is a children's dental care platform that transforms brushing into a fun adventure. We combine an interactive app with kid-friendly dental products to build healthy habits early.

What age group is Gummy Clouds designed for?

Gummy Clouds is designed for children ages 2 to 12. The app adjusts its content and difficulty level based on your child's age to keep things engaging and age-appropriate.

Is Gummy Clouds available internationally?

Yes! The Gummy Clouds app is available worldwide on iOS and Android. Our dental care products currently ship to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom, with more countries coming soon.

How do I get started?

Simply download the Gummy Clouds app, create a free account, and set up your child's profile. You can start using the brushing timer and educational content immediately. Dental care products can be ordered separately.

Products & Safety

Are your products safe for children?

Absolutely. All Gummy Clouds products are pediatric dentist-approved, free from harmful chemicals, and meet the highest safety standards. Our toothpastes use safe, effective fluoride levels recommended by the ADA.

What ingredients are in Gummy Clouds toothpaste?

Our toothpaste uses naturally derived ingredients including xylitol, fluoride (1000 ppm for ages 2-6, 1450 ppm for ages 6+), silica, and natural fruit flavors. No artificial colors, parabens, or SLS.

Do you offer fluoride-free options?

Yes, we offer a hydroxyapatite-based fluoride-free option for families who prefer it. This formula is equally effective at remineralizing enamel and is safe if accidentally swallowed.

Are the products cruelty-free and eco-friendly?

Yes! All Gummy Clouds products are cruelty-free and never tested on animals. Our packaging is made from recycled materials and is fully recyclable. Our toothbrush handles are made from bamboo or recycled plastic.

What flavors are available?

Our toothpaste comes in four kid-tested flavors: Strawberry Cloud, Bubblegum Burst, Watermelon Wave, and Mild Mint (for older kids). All flavors are naturally derived and sugar-free.

Subscriptions & Billing

What plans do you offer?

We offer three plans: Free (basic timer and tracking), Family ($9.99/month for premium app features and teledentistry), and Complete ($19.99/month for app + monthly dental care product deliveries).

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel anytime from your account settings. There are no cancellation fees. You'll continue to have access to your plan until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes! All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You can explore all premium features and decide if Gummy Clouds is right for your family.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Dental Health

How often should my child brush their teeth?

The American Dental Association recommends brushing twice a day for two minutes each time. Our app timer helps ensure your child brushes for the full recommended duration with fun animations.

When should I take my child to the dentist for the first time?

The ADA recommends a child's first dental visit by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth appearing. Our teledentistry feature can help with early consultations.

Can Gummy Clouds replace regular dental check-ups?

No. Gummy Clouds is designed to complement regular dental care, not replace it. Our teledentistry consultations are great for quick questions and monitoring, but children still need regular in-person dental check-ups every six months.

How does the teledentistry feature work?

With our Family or Complete plan, you can schedule secure video consultations with licensed pediatric dentists. Simply book a time slot, and a dentist will guide you through an oral health check using your phone's camera.

App & Technology

What devices does the app support?

Gummy Clouds is available on iOS (iPhone and iPad running iOS 15+) and Android (devices running Android 10+). We also have a web version accessible from any modern browser.

Is my child's data safe?

Absolutely. We are fully COPPA compliant and take children's privacy extremely seriously. We use end-to-end encryption, never sell personal data, and give parents full control over their child's information. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I add multiple children to one account?

Yes! You can add up to 5 children per account. Each child gets their own cloud buddy, personalized routine, and progress tracking. All managed from a single parent dashboard.

Does the app work offline?

The brushing timer and basic tracking features work offline. Once you reconnect, your data syncs automatically. Teledentistry and some educational content require an internet connection.

Your dental health journey

Four simple steps to healthier smiles for the whole family.

1

Download

Get the app and create your family profile

Download Gummy Clouds from your app store, set up your family account, and add each child's profile. It takes less than two minutes.

2

Customize

Choose your cloud buddy and set goals

Each child picks their own cloud companion. Set brushing goals, reminders, and reward milestones tailored to their age and needs.

3

Brush

Start brushing with interactive guides

Your cloud buddy guides each brushing session with a fun two-minute timer, showing which areas to brush and cheering kids along the way.

4

Track

Monitor progress and earn rewards

Track streaks, earn gummy stars, and unlock achievements. Parents get real-time insights into each child's dental health habits.

Pricing FAQs

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to the new features. When downgrading, the change takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

How does the free trial work?

The Family and Premium plans include a 30-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial ends. You can cancel anytime during the trial with no obligation.

What happens when I cancel?

When you cancel, you keep access to your plan's features until the end of your billing cycle. After that, your account reverts to the free Marshmallow plan. Your data and progress are never deleted.

Do you offer discounts for schools or clinics?

Yes! We offer volume discounts for schools, dental clinics, and pediatric practices. Contact us for custom pricing tailored to your organization's needs.

How does teledentistry work on the Premium plan?

Premium members can book live video consultations with licensed pediatric dentists directly through the app. Sessions include interactive dental exams, personalized advice, and follow-up summaries sent to your dashboard.
